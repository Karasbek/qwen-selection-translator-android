Upstream English (US) data is based on a modified cmudict-ipa:
https://github.com/menelik3/cmudict-ipa
MIT license, copyright 2016 Lingliang Zhang (LICENSE-cmudict-ipa.txt).
The underlying CMU Pronouncing Dictionary notice is retained in LICENSE-CMU.txt.
Upstream credits https://github.com/kylebgorman/syllabify for generating stress markings.
No syllabification code is shipped or executed here.

Only the English (US) dataset is bundled. Other ipa-dict language datasets and
their licenses do not apply to this asset. Rows were sorted by case-insensitive
word for binary search; words and IPA were not changed. Source and bundled
checksums, entry count and size are in provenance.json. All these notices ship
in the APK alongside the dictionary.
